You are more than a checklist of symptoms.

At Avanelle, we slow the process down enough to make it useful. We look at symptoms, functioning, health history, relationships, sleep, stress, culture, and the goals that matter to you. Then we make decisions together.

Good questions matter

Before your first visit

Is Avanelle a physician practice?

No. Avanelle is a psychiatric-mental health advanced practice nursing practice led by Cynthia Love, APRN, MSN, PMHNP-BC. Services are provided within applicable state licensure, prescriptive-authority, and professional nursing requirements.

Are visits fully online?

Yes. Avanelle is designed as a telehealth-first practice. You must be physically located in a state where your clinician is licensed at the time of the visit. Availability may vary by state and service.

Will medication always be prescribed?

No. Medication is one possible part of care, not a guaranteed outcome. Recommendations follow a complete assessment, shared decision-making, clinical appropriateness, and applicable laws.

Does Avanelle prescribe controlled medication?

Controlled medications may be considered only when clinically appropriate and legally permitted. Decisions require clear treatment goals, careful monitoring, and regular reassessment. A first visit never guarantees a prescription.

Can Avanelle help in a crisis?

Avanelle does not provide emergency or crisis services. If you may harm yourself or someone else, call 911, call or text 988, or go to the nearest emergency department.
